# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Play soft feedback for right clicks (including two-finger and corner clicks) and other mouse buttons.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Replaces the built-in Force Touch trackpad's click with the same press and release pulses at a lower strength.
- Strength from 10% to 100% in 10% steps, 30% by default.
- Restores the stock click when turned off, on quit, sleep, a locked screen, and on the next launch after a crash.
- Launch at login.
- `--diagnose`, `--restore` and `--selftest` command line options.
- English and Russian interface.
- Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel.
